About This Project

Welcome to my computational physics portfolio. This website document my weekly exercises and investigation into Two-dimentional transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs).

My interest include strain engineering, exciton dynamics, exciton-phono interactions, and first-principles calculations.

Research Topics

Strain Engineering

Investigating how mechanical strain changes the properties of TMDs.

Exciton Physics

Studying exciton binding energies and relaxation mechanisms.

First-Principles Modelling

Exploring density functional theory and numerical simulations.

Exciton-Phonon Coupling

Investigating interactions between excitons and lattice vibrations.
